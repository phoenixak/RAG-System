# Lanternfish Beacon — Customer Refund and Cancellation Policy

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

This policy applies to subscriptions of **Beacon**, our analytics platform, bought directly from Lanternfish Analytics. Purchases made through a reseller or a cloud marketplace follow that partner's terms.

## 1. Monthly plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time from the billing page. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. We do **not** refund partial months on monthly plans.

## 2. Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded **in full within 30 days** of the start date (or of the renewal date). After those 30 days, if you cancel an annual plan we refund the unused months **on a prorated basis**, minus a cancellation fee of **10% of the remaining amount**. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within **14 days**.

## 3. Enterprise contracts

Enterprise contracts (more than 250 seats or with a custom DPA) are not covered by this policy. Their cancellation and refund terms are set in the signed order form, and changes must go through your account manager.

## 4. Service credits

If Beacon misses its uptime commitment in a given month, eligible customers receive service credits instead of refunds — see the Support and SLA document for the credit table. Credits must be claimed within **30 days** of the end of the affected month.

## 5. Trials

The free trial lasts **14 days** and does not require a credit card. Trials are not charged, so they cannot be refunded.
