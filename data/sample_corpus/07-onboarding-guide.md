# Lanternfish Analytics — Onboarding Guide

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped to your home address **5 working days** before your start date. You receive your company email and SSO invitation two days before you start. Please set up multi-factor authentication before your first morning — the Security Policy requires it on every account.

## Your first week

- **Day 1:** welcome call with People Operations at 10:00 local time, laptop setup with IT, and lunch (on us, up to USD 30) with your onboarding buddy.
- **Day 2:** product walkthrough of Beacon and an introduction to the data platform.
- **Day 3:** security and privacy training. It is mandatory and must be completed within your **first 5 working days**.
- **Days 4–5:** shadow customer calls and pick a starter task with your manager.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new joiner gets a buddy from another team for their first **90 days**. Your buddy is your first stop for questions about how things work here; they are not your manager.

## 30-60-90 plan

Within your first two weeks you agree a 30-60-90 day plan with your manager. Your probation period is **3 months** for all roles; the review happens in the last two weeks of probation.
