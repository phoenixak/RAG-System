# Lanternfish Analytics — Employee Handbook

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

## 1. Working hours

Our core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone**, Monday to Thursday. Outside those hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as you are reachable for scheduled meetings. Fridays are **focus days**: no internal meetings may be booked on a Friday unless there is a live customer incident.

The standard working week is 40 hours. Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance and is compensated as time off in lieu at a rate of 1.5 hours per extra hour worked.

## 2. Remote and hybrid work

Lanternfish is remote-first. Employees may work from any country where we have a legal entity (Egypt, Germany, Portugal, the United Kingdom and the United States). Working from another country for more than **30 consecutive days** requires written approval from People Operations, because of tax and immigration rules.

Every employee receives a one-time **home-office allowance of USD 800** in their first month, plus **USD 40 per month** towards internet costs. Hub offices in Lisbon and Cairo can be booked through the office app; desks are released at 11:00 if nobody has checked in.

## 3. Paid time off

- **Annual leave:** 25 working days per calendar year, on top of local public holidays. Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- **Sick leave:** 12 paid days per year. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.
- **Parental leave:** 20 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver, which can be taken any time in the first 12 months.
- **Volunteering:** 2 paid days per year for registered charity work.

Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least **two weeks** in advance for anything longer than three days.

## 4. Performance and growth

Performance conversations happen twice a year, in **April and October**. Each employee has an annual **learning budget of USD 1,500** for courses, books and conferences; unused budget does not carry over. Promotions are reviewed in the October cycle only.

## 5. Equipment

New joiners choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every **three years**. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within **24 hours** (see the Security Policy for the incident process).
