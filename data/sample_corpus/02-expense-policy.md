# Lanternfish Analytics — Expense Policy

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

## 1. General rules

Expenses must be necessary for work, reasonable and supported by an itemised receipt. Claims are submitted in the finance app and must be filed within **60 days** of the purchase date; claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval, normally within **10 working days**.

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Amount (per claim) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 250 | Automatic, if the receipt matches the category |
| USD 250 – 2,000 | Your line manager |
| Over USD 2,000 | Line manager **and** the Finance Director |

Software subscriptions of any amount need IT approval first, because of the security review in the Security Policy.

## 3. Meals and entertainment

- Working meals while travelling: covered by the daily per diem (see the Travel Policy).
- Client entertainment: up to **USD 90 per person**, and the names of the attendees must be listed on the claim.
- Team celebrations: up to **USD 45 per person**, at most four times a year per team.
- Alcohol is only reimbursable as part of client entertainment.

## 4. What is not reimbursable

Traffic and parking fines, personal grooming, upgrades to premium seats that are not allowed by the Travel Policy, airline lounge passes, and gifts to colleagues.

## 5. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year can request a corporate card. Card statements must be reconciled within **15 days** of the statement date. Cash withdrawals with the corporate card are not allowed.
