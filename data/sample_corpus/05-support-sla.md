# Lanternfish Beacon — Support and Service Level Agreement

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

## 1. Uptime commitment

Beacon is available **99.9%** of the time in every calendar month, measured at our API gateway. Scheduled maintenance, announced at least **72 hours** in advance, does not count as downtime. Maintenance windows are on Sundays between 02:00 and 05:00 UTC.

## 2. Service credits

| Monthly uptime | Credit (share of that month's fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.9% | 10% |
| 95.0% – 99.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Credits are applied to the next invoice and are never paid out as cash. To claim them, see section 4 of the Refund and Cancellation Policy.

## 3. Support severity levels

| Severity | Meaning | First response (Business plan) | First response (Enterprise plan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Beacon is down or data is at risk | 1 hour, 24/7 | 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| Sev 2 | A major feature is broken, no workaround | 4 business hours | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| Sev 3 | A feature is impaired, a workaround exists | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| Sev 4 | Questions and feature requests | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday. The Starter plan gets email support only, with no response-time commitment.

## 4. How to contact support

Open a ticket at help.lanternfish.example or email support@lanternfish.example. Enterprise customers also get a shared Slack channel and a named customer success manager. Sev 1 issues must be reported by phone through the emergency line listed in your order form.
