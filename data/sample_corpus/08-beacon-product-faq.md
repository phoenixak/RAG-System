# Lanternfish Beacon — Product FAQ

*Fictional product. All names, numbers and features are invented for the RAG demo.*

## What is Beacon?

Beacon is a product-analytics platform. It collects events from your web and mobile apps, builds funnels and retention charts, and lets you ask questions about your product data in plain English.

## Plans

| Plan | Seats | Monthly events | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Up to 5 | 1 million | USD 49 / month |
| Business | Up to 250 | 50 million | USD 18 per seat / month |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Custom | Custom contract |

Annual billing gives a **15% discount** on Starter and Business.

## Where is my data stored?

You choose a data region when you create a workspace: **EU (Frankfurt)** or **US (Oregon)**. Data never leaves the chosen region, and the region cannot be changed after the workspace is created.

## How long do you keep data?

Raw events are kept for **13 months** on Starter and Business, and for **25 months** on Enterprise. Aggregated reports are kept for as long as your account is active. When an account is closed, all data is deleted within **30 days**.

## Single sign-on

SAML single sign-on and SCIM user provisioning are available on the **Enterprise** plan. Business plans support Google and Microsoft sign-in.

## API limits

The ingestion API accepts up to **1,000 events per second** per workspace. The query API is limited to **60 requests per minute** per API key; Enterprise workspaces can ask for higher limits.

## Can I get a refund?

See the Refund and Cancellation Policy. In short, annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days and prorated after that; monthly plans are not refunded for partial months.
