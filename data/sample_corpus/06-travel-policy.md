# Lanternfish Analytics — Travel Policy

*Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies are invented for the RAG demo.*

## 1. Booking

All business travel is booked through the travel desk in the finance app at least **14 days** before departure where possible. Trips need manager approval before booking; international trips also need approval from the department head.

## 2. Flights

Economy class is the default. **Premium economy** is allowed on flights longer than **6 hours**, and **business class** only on flights longer than **10 hours** with the Finance Director's approval. Paid seat selection up to USD 40 per flight is fine.

## 3. Hotels

Nightly hotel caps, including taxes:

| City group | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| London, New York, San Francisco | USD 320 |
| Lisbon, Berlin, Cairo | USD 180 |
| Everywhere else | USD 150 |

Stays longer than 7 nights should use a serviced apartment if it is cheaper.

## 4. Per diem

While travelling you receive a **daily per diem of USD 70** for meals and incidentals (USD 95 in London, New York and San Francisco). The per diem replaces individual meal receipts. If a meal is provided by the event or the client, deduct USD 20 for that meal.

## 5. Ground transport

Use public transport or standard ride-hailing. Car rental needs approval and is limited to compact or mid-size cars. Mileage for using your own car is reimbursed at **USD 0.55 per kilometre**.

## 6. Safety

Register every international trip with the travel desk so that we can reach you in an emergency. Our travel assistance line is available 24/7 at +1 555 0100 (fictional).
